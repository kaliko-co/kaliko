Add a supplement

Type the numbers straight off the label, for one dose — one capsule, tablet or drop. Leave anything not printed on your label blank.

If your label gives IU instead of µg or mg: for vitamin D, divide by 40; for vitamin A, divide by about 3.3.

A few things sometimes on a label have no home here yet and won't be saved: niacin, pantothenic acid, biotin, vitamin K, and non-nutrient ingredients like citicoline or herbal extracts.
